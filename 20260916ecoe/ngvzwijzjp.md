# The holding of the Reproductive Alliance Conference

> 更新时间：2026-09-16 (UTC+8)

Today the 3rd Yellow River Delta Reproductive Alliance Conference and the 6th Yellow River Delta Reproductive Endocrinology Conference was  grandly held in Zhanhua ,a beautiful place where the autumn air is crisp and the jujube fragrance is overflowing. With the help of Binzhou Second People's Hospital, Zhanhua Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine and Zhanhua Second People's Hospital, department of Reproductive Medicine of Binzhou Medical University Hospital sponsored  the grand meeting in James Joyce Cofftel.

We had honor to invite Professor Zhu Guijin (Tongji medical College of Hust), Professor Deng Chengyan (Peking Union Medical College Hospital), Professor Deng Xiaohui（ Qilu Hospital of Shandong University）, Professor Sun Zhengao, Professor Zhao Shuqin, Professor Yang Aijun and Professor Ma Huagang,Professor Liu Tao and other  leaders from the alliance units attending our conference .

Professor Wang Yanlin, director of the Yellow River Delta Reproductive Medicine Alliance, director of the Department of Reproductive Medicine of Binzhou Medical University Hospital and chairman of the conference, delivered an opening speech, expressing her  gratitude and touching to all the experts and colleagues. Since the establishment of the Reproductive Medicine Alliance in the Yellow River Delta,Each alliance unit try their best to enhance exchanges and cooperation around " tiered medical services, disease prevention and control, personnel training, technical guidance" and other aspects. Thank you all for your efforts. We will work together to take responsibility for solve the problem of infertility in the Yellow River Delta all the way .

_
At the end of autumn and the beginning of winter, the temperature dropped sharply today, but the enthusiasm of the participants and the academic sharing of experts made us feel warm and happy.

_

Professor Zhu Guijin gave a special lecture on the significance of hormone determination, who emphasized the unreasonable determination and misreading of hormones in clinical work, and updated the latest concept of hormone interpretation.

_

Professor Deng Xiaohui gave an  excellent lecture on the diagnosis and treatment of recurrent abortion, and shared her clinical experience in the diagnosis and treatment of recurrent abortion patients.

_

Professor Ma Huagang called on detailed interpretation of the diagnosis and treatment of pituitary neuroendocrine tumors in reproductive medicine.

_

Professor Zhao Shuqin gave instruction on Expert Consensus on Diagnosis and Treatment of Unexplained Infertility, and shared her  experience in diagnosis and treatment in clinical work.

Professor Li Xuefeng gave a professional lecture on the treatment of male infertility with diabetes mellitus.Professor Sheng Yuguo gave a special lecture on Pregnancy and Venous Thromboembolic Diseases.Professor.Professor Sun Zhenggao gave a special lecture on the treatment of premature ovarian insufficiency (POI) with integrated traditional Chinese and Western medicine.Professor Gao Jinxiang gave a special lecture on the key points of diagnosis and treatment of pregnancy with metabolic disorders.Professor Wang Xinhua gave a special lecture on the application progress of ultrasonic hysterosalpingography. Professor Deng Chengyan gave an online lecture on the influence of immunotherapy on IVF pregnancy outcome, guiding and standardizating the treatment criteria. Professor Ning Fangling gave a lecture on the treatment of reproductive tumors online.

The conference came to an end in a rich academic feast and heated discussion.

Finally, Professor Zhao Huaping, the director  of Binzhou Second People's Hospital delivered the closing speech. She said: The day passed quickly .We swam in the academic ocean, immersed in the lectures of experts, and recalled their unique insights. All of us benefit greatly from the conference ! It is hoped that through the academic sharing of experts today, the level of diagnosis and treatment about reproductive diseases in the Yellow River Delta will be further improved. This conference pave the way for cooperation among alliance , so as  benefit the infertility patients in the Yellow River Delta region.
